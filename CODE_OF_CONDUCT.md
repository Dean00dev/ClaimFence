# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique claims and code rather than people.
- Welcome counterexamples and good-faith corrections.
- Avoid publishing private or security-sensitive information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when behaviour is
harassing, threatening, discriminatory, or otherwise harmful. Report conduct concerns to
the maintainer through a private channel listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and fairly as practicable.

This compact policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
